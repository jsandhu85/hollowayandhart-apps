        The order the extra goes in

          How the dates are worked out

          Each month, interest is added to every balance (APR ÷ 12), then every minimum is paid, then whatever is left of your monthly amount goes to the next debt in line until it's gone. A debt is paid off in the month its balance reaches zero, and its minimum moves on to the next debt. It's an estimate: your lender's dates and rounding may differ a little.

              Help

              Where your information is kept

              Everything you type stays in this browser on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere, and the app works with no internet. There is no account and no subscription.

              Keep a backup

              Settings → Back up now saves a small .json file to your downloads. Do it every few weeks. The app reminds you if it has been 30 days.

              If you clear your browser's history or site data, the app's information goes with it. A backup brings it back: Settings → Restore a backup.

              Open it the same way each time

              Each browser keeps its own copy. If you open the app in two different browsers, you get two separate copies. Pick one way to open it and keep to it. Restore your backup if it ever opens empty.

              A new phone or computer

              On the old device: Settings → Back up now. Move the backup file to the new device (email it to yourself, or use a cloud drive), open the app there, then Settings → Restore a backup.

              The numbers

              Each month, interest is added to every balance (APR ÷ 12, to the cent), every minimum is paid, and the rest of your monthly amount goes to one debt at a time: the smallest balance first (Snowball), the highest APR first (Avalanche), or your own order. When a debt is paid off, its minimum moves on to the next one.

              Paying only the minimums: each debt on its own minimum, with nothing moved on when one is paid off. Interest saved is the difference between that and your plan. What if: the same plan with your extra added each month.

              Payments: each one comes off that debt's balance, or sets the balance you type from your statement. Paid off so far = where your debts started minus what you owe now.

              This app is a planning tool for your own numbers. It isn't financial advice.
