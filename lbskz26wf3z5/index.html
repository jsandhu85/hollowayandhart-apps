                    Help

                    Where your client book is kept

                    Everything you type stays in this browser on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere, and the app works with no internet. There is no account and no subscription.

                    Keep client details private

                    Your clients trust you with their phone numbers, birthdays and what they tell you about allergies. Keep this device locked, and keep backup files somewhere only you can open them.

                    The app keeps what you type, exactly as you type it. It doesn't check, judge or suggest anything about a client.

                    Keep a backup

                    Settings → Back up now saves a small .json file. Do it every few weeks. The app reminds you if it has been 30 days.

                    If you clear your browser's history or site data, the app's information goes with it. A backup brings it back: Settings → Restore a backup.

                    Open it the same way each time

                    Each browser keeps its own copy. If you open the app in two different browsers, you get two separate client books. Pick one and stick with it. Restore your backup if the app ever opens empty.

                    A new phone or computer

                    On the old device: Settings → Back up now. Open the app on the new device, then Settings → Restore a backup.

                    How the app works things out

                    Rebook: due back = the day of the client's last done appointment + that service's rebook weeks. Clients with a booking from today on aren't listed. No-shows and cancellations don't count as a visit.

                    Patch tests: the latest test for each product or area, when its next-due date is within two weeks or has passed.

                    Income: done appointments (their price), tips, and the product sales you log, by week or month. Booked, no-show and cancelled appointments count nothing.

                    Overlapping appointments get a note, never a block: you know your day best.
