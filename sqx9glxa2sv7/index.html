                        Help

                        Where your information is kept

                        Everything you type stays in this browser on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere, and the app works with no internet. There is no account and no subscription.

                        Keep a backup

                        Settings → Back up now saves a small .json file. Do it every few weeks. The app reminds you if it has been 30 days.

                        If you clear your browser's history or site data, the app's information goes with it. A backup brings it back: Settings → Restore a backup.

                        Open it the same way each time

                        Each browser keeps its own copy. If you open the app in two different browsers, you get two separate copies. Pick one way of opening it and stay with it: in some browsers a moved or renamed file opens empty. Restore your backup if that happens.

                        A new phone or computer

                        On the old device: Settings → Back up now. Copy the backup file to the new device, open the app there, then Settings → Restore a backup.

                        A new year

                        Settings → Which year the app shows. Every year's plan and entries are kept. On the Months tab, 'Copy last year's plan' starts the new year from the old one.

                        The numbers

                        Left over = the income you've logged in the year − the spending you've logged in the year. Each month: what came in and went out against that month's plan.

                        Sinking funds: what's left to save ÷ the whole months until the month it's due (at least 1), rounded up to the cent. Goals: what's left ÷ the months to the goal's date. Net worth: what you own minus what you owe.

                        Year review: the strongest and tightest months are picked from the months with something logged, leaving out the month you're in until it's over.

                        This app is a planning tool for your own numbers. It isn't financial advice.
