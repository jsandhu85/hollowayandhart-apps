              Help

              Where your library is kept

              Everything you type stays in this browser on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere, and the app works with no internet. There is no account and no subscription.

              Keep a backup

              Settings → Back up now saves a small .json file to your downloads. Do it every few weeks. The app reminds you if it has been 30 days.

              If you clear your browser's history or site data, the app's library goes with it. A backup brings it back: Settings → Restore a backup.

              Open it the same way each time

              Each browser keeps its own copy. If you open the app in two different browsers, you get two separate copies. Pick one browser and keep the app file in one place: in some browsers a moved or renamed file opens empty. Restore your backup if that happens.

              A new phone or computer

              On the old device: Settings → Back up now. Copy the app file and the backup file to the new device, open the app there, then Settings → Restore a backup.

              Importing a Goodreads or StoryGraph export

              Download your export file from your Goodreads or StoryGraph account (the Import screen and the guide give the steps), then Import → Choose a CSV file. The app shows how many books are new and how many are already in your library (matched by title and author), then adds the new ones. Nothing is removed. Tick 'Update matches' if you want the file to change the status, rating and dates of books you already have.

              The app reads the file you give it; it doesn't look anything up online and isn't connected to those sites.

              The challenge and the stats

              The challenge counts books with the status Finished and a finish date in this year. 'On pace' means you've finished at least your goal × the day of the year ÷ the days in the year. Stats count finished books by their finish month.
