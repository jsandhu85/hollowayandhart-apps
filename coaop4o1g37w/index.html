                        Help

                        Where your information is kept

                        Everything you type stays in this browser on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere, and the app works with no internet. There is no account and no subscription. The app doesn't connect to any selling platform: you type your items and sales in yourself.

                        Keep a backup

                        Settings → Back up now saves a small .json file to your downloads. Do it every few weeks. The app reminds you if it has been 30 days.

                        If you clear your browser's history or site data, the app's information goes with it. A backup brings it back: Settings → Restore a backup.

                        Open it the same way each time

                        Each browser keeps its own copy. If you open the app in two different browsers, you get two separate copies. Pick one and keep using it. Restore your backup if it ever opens empty.

                        A new phone or computer

                        On the old device: Settings → Back up now. Copy the backup file to the new device, open the app there, then Settings → Restore a backup.

                        The numbers

                        Profit on an item = sale price + shipping charged to the buyer − platform fees − shipping you paid − what you paid for the item.

                        Fees = the platform's % of the sale price (plus the shipping charged, if that platform takes its % from shipping too) + its fixed fee per order, rounded to the cent. The fee is worked out when you record the sale and kept with it, so changing a rate later changes new sales only. You can always type the fee your platform actually charged.

                        A month's numbers are every item sold that month: sales (sale price + shipping charged), fees, shipping you paid, cost of the items and profit. Mileage = each trip's distance × its rate; profit after mileage = profit − mileage.

                        Ageing: days since an item was listed, in groups of 0–30 days, 31–90 days and over 90 days.

                        Fee rates

                        The rates the app starts with are examples: check your platform's current fees and type your own in Settings → Platforms and fees. Add any other place you sell, such as a market stall.

                        The CSV

                        Items → Export items as CSV saves every item as a spreadsheet file: dates, platform, prices, shipping, fees, profit and notes. Handy at the end of the year.

                        This app is a record of your own numbers. It isn't tax advice.
