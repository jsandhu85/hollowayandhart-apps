              Help

              A record-keeping tool

              It's a record-keeping tool. It isn't tax advice. The tax figures, the profit and the mileage estimate are worked out from what you type, so they are only as right as the entries. Your tax office or an accountant can tell you what applies to your business.

              Where your information is kept

              Everything you type stays in this browser on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere, and the app works with no internet. There is no account and no subscription.

              Keep a backup

              Settings → Back up now saves a small .json file with everything in it. Do it every few weeks. The app reminds you if it has been 30 days.

              If you clear your browser's history or site data, the app's information goes with it. A backup brings it back: Settings → Restore a backup.

              Open it the same way each time

              Each browser keeps its own copy. If you open the app in two different browsers, you get two separate copies. Pick one way to open it and keep to it: in some browsers a moved or renamed computer copy opens empty. Restore your backup if that happens.

              A new phone or computer

              On the old device: Settings → Back up now. Move the backup file to the new device (email it to yourself, or use a cloud drive), open the app there, then Settings → Restore a backup.

              The tax figures

              Australia: for an entry ticked 'GST included', the GST is the amount ÷ 11, rounded to the cent. BAS quarters run Jul–Sep, Oct–Dec, Jan–Mar and Apr–Jun in a financial year from 1 July to 30 June.

              United States and United Kingdom: for an entry ticked 'Sales tax included' or 'VAT included', the tax is the amount × rate ÷ (100 + rate), rounded to the cent, at the rate in Settings. Sales tax quarters follow the calendar year; VAT quarters start in the month you pick.

              For each quarter the app adds up the tax in your income (collected) and in your expenses (paid), and shows the difference. These are estimates from what you typed. They aren't a tax return or a bill.

              Profit and loss

              Every entry counts at its amount without the tax (for entries that are ticked). Profit = income − expenses, for the whole year or any month. The year is the Australian financial year (1 Jul – 30 Jun), the calendar year in the United States, or the UK tax year (6 Apr – 5 Apr).

              Mileage

              Type each business trip's distance. The year's estimate is the total distance × your rate, rounded to the cent. It isn't added into Profit, so a vehicle cost is never counted twice.

              CSV export

              Entries → Export entries as CSV saves your entries (all years, or one year) as a file any spreadsheet opens: the day, income or expense, category, description, amount, the tax part, the amount without tax and the receipt note. Export trips as CSV does the same for your trips.
