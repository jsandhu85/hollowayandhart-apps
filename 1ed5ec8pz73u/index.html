              How the dates are worked out

              Each month, interest is added to every balance (APR ÷ 12), then every minimum is paid, then whatever is left of your monthly amount goes to the next debt in line until it's gone. A debt is paid off in the month its balance reaches zero. It's an estimate: your lender's dates and rounding may differ a little.

                      Help

                      Where your information is kept

                      Everything you type stays in this browser on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere, and the app works with no internet. There is no account and no subscription.

                      Keep a backup

                      Settings → Back up now saves a small .json file to your downloads. Do it every few weeks. The app reminds you if it has been 30 days.

                      If you clear your browser's history or site data, the app's information goes with it. A backup brings it back: Settings → Restore a backup.

                      Open it the same way each time

                      Each browser keeps its own copy. If you open the app in two different browsers, you get two separate copies. Pick one browser and keep the app file in one place: in some browsers a moved or renamed file opens empty. Restore your backup if that happens.

                      A new phone or computer

                      On the old device: Settings → Back up now. Copy the app file and the backup file to the new device, open the app there, then Settings → Restore a backup.

                      The numbers

                      Safe to Spend = your pay − the bills due this pay − what you put aside − what you've spent so far this pay. A day = Safe to Spend shared over the days left until payday.

                      Budget: each category's planned amount for the month against what you've logged in it. Debts: interest is added monthly (APR ÷ 12), the minimums are paid, and the rest of your monthly amount goes to one debt at a time, smallest balance first (Snowball) or highest rate first (Avalanche). Goals: what's left ÷ the months to your date. Net worth: what you own minus what you owe.

                      This app is a planning tool for your own numbers. It isn't financial advice.
