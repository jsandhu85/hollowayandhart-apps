                      Help

                      Where your recipes are kept

                      Everything you type stays in this browser on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere, and the app works with no internet. There is no account and no subscription.

                      Keep a backup

                      Settings → Back up now saves a small .json file. Do it every few weeks. The app reminds you if it has been 30 days.

                      If you clear your browser's history or site data, the app's recipes go with it. A backup brings them back: Settings → Restore a backup.

                      Open it the same way each time

                      Each browser keeps its own copy. If you open the app in two different browsers, you get two separate copies. Pick one and keep using it. Restore your backup if the app ever opens empty.

                      A new phone or computer

                      On the old device: Settings → Back up now. Move the backup file to the new device (email it to yourself, or use a cloud drive), open the app there, then Settings → Restore a backup.

                      Amounts you can type

                      Whole numbers (2), decimals with a point (1.5), fractions (1/2), mixed numbers (1 1/2) and fraction characters copied from a recipe (½, ¾). The Amount box opens the keyboard with / and a space. Leave the amount blank for things like 'salt to taste'. Units are yours to type: g, ml, cups, tbsp, tins.

                      Servings

                      Each amount is multiplied by the servings you want and divided by what the recipe serves. A recipe for 4 cooked for 6 uses 1.5 times everything. Amounts are shown to 2 decimal places at most (0.375 shows as 0.38). At the recipe's own servings you see the amounts just as you typed them.

                      How the grocery list is worked out

                      The list covers the 7 days of the week shown. Every planned meal from a recipe adds the recipe's ingredients, scaled to that meal's servings. The same item in the same unit is added into one line: 'onion' and 'Onions' match, and so do 'cup' and 'cups'. The same item in different units stays on separate lines, because the app doesn't convert units (cups to grams, ml to litres).

                      Lines are grouped by aisle, in your order from Settings. Typed meals, such as Leftovers, add nothing. Ticks stay with the week until you clear them.

                      The pantry

                      If your pantry has the item in the same unit, the app takes that amount off. If that covers it, the line moves to 'Covered by your pantry'. If the pantry has it in another unit or with no amount, the line moves to 'Check your pantry' so you can look before you shop.

                      Copy last week

                      On the Planner, Copy last week puts every meal from the week before onto the same days of the week shown. If the week already has meals, the app asks first, and the meals already there stay.
